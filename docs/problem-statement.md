# STYLOOM – Problem Statement

## The Problem

Traditional custom tailoring often requires customers to visit tailoring shops multiple times, communicate their preferences manually and repeatedly follow up on their orders.

Customers may face difficulties explaining their exact clothing requirements, while tailors may find it challenging to organise customer measurements, customisation requests and multiple orders efficiently.

## Who Is Affected?

- Customers looking for customised clothing.
- Local tailors and tailoring shops.
- Small boutique businesses.

## Why Does It Matter?

The traditional process can be time-consuming and may lead to communication gaps, incorrect customisation and difficulty tracking order progress.

## Existing Gap

Many tailoring services still depend heavily on physical visits and manual communication. STYLOOM aims to make this process more convenient through a digital platform.
