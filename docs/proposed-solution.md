# STYLOOM – Proposed Solution

## Our Solution

STYLOOM is a digital custom-tailoring platform designed to connect customers with local tailors and boutiques.

It aims to simplify the process of ordering customised clothing by allowing customers to select garments, choose fabrics and designs, submit measurements and track their order progress online.

## How STYLOOM Works

1. Customer visits the STYLOOM platform.
2. Customer selects a garment category.
3. Customer chooses preferred fabric, colour and design.
4. Customer enters measurements and customisation details.
5. Customer places a tailoring order.
6. Tailor receives and manages the order.
7. Customer checks the order status.

## Key Features

- **Garment Selection:** Explore different clothing categories.
- **Fabric Selection:** Choose preferred fabrics and colours.
- **Customisation:** Provide personal design preferences.
- **Measurement Form:** Submit body measurements.
- **Tailor Dashboard:** Manage customer orders.
- **Order Tracking:** Check tailoring progress.

## 24-Hour Hackathon MVP

The initial version aims to demonstrate:

- Customer-facing website.
- Garment and fabric selection.
- Measurement submission form.
- Basic order placement.
- Tailor-side order management.
- Order status display.

## Future Enhancements

- AI-assisted measurement guidance.
- Virtual garment previews.
- Smart tailor recommendations.

## Expected Benefits

STYLOOM aims to make custom tailoring more convenient for customers and help local tailors organise and manage their orders digitally.
